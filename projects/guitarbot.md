## GuitarBot Jams

GuitarBot is a robotic guitarist at Georgia Tech's Robotic Musicianship laboratory. Upon joining the lab, Ryan Baker was instantly drawn to the project.

However, there was no way to interactively play along with GuitarBot yet. Baker developed a backend that allowed a user to play audio into a DAW, which is then automatically transcribed into chord information for GuitarBot to improvise over. This is part of ongoing research at Georgia Tech.
